EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

1. Problem Statement & Campus Micro-Climate Context

Why city-wide macro AQI monitors fail to capture micro-local inversions in high-density educational environments.

Target Context: JIIT Noida Sector 62

Located at 28.6295° N, 77.3715° E, the campus accommodates over 4,500 students and staff in a high-density urban footprint immediately adjacent to two severe pollution emission corridors.

Corridor A: NH-24 Highway Corridor (45° NE)

Heavy vehicular diesel corridor carrying over 150,000 vehicles daily, generating relentless road dust (PM₁₀) and tailpipe particulate matter (PM₂.₅) directly along Jaypee Road.

Corridor B: Sector 63 Industrial Zone (135° SE)

Industrial fabrication and generator complex situated Southeast of campus across Vishwakarma Road, contributing intermittent chemical smog during stagnant hours.

Winter Thermal Inversion Stagnation

During NCR winter months (Nov–Feb), shallow boundary layer inversions trap pollutants below 150m altitude. Calm winds (<1.5 m/s) and high humidity (>80%) trigger severe micro-local smog spikes.

PHASE 01 ARCHITECTURE

2. Real-Time Data Pipeline & Spatial Feature Vector Engine

Automated ingestion from Open-Meteo APIs coupled with spatial alignment vector engineering.

Ingestion Pipeline (`realtime_pipeline.py`)

  • Live Ingestion: Connects to Open-Meteo Air Quality & Weather APIs for Sector 62 coordinates.
  • Parameters Fetched: Outdoor PM₂.₅, PM₁₀, NO₂, Temperature (2m), Humidity (2m), Wind Speed (10m), Wind Direction (10m).
  • Offline Fallback: Automatic failover to historical Noida dataset logs (`cleaned_air_quality_dataset.xlsx`) if offline.

Spatial Vector Engineering (`feature_pipeline.py`)

Alignment Vector = Max ( 0 , Cos( Wind Angle − Corridor Angle ) )
  • NH-24 Corridor Alignment (45° NE): Measures wind blowing directly from highway.
  • Industrial Corridor Alignment (135° SE): Measures wind from Sector 63 factories.
  • Stagnation Index Formula: Combines low wind speed (<1.5 m/s), high humidity (>80%), and low temperature.
